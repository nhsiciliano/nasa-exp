# 3-orbita

Fuente editable: prompt usado con la herramienta integrada `imagegen`.
Generación independiente, sin imágenes de referencia y con fondo opaco.

Proceso: generar con el prompt inicial, aplicar el ajuste de encuadre sobre esa imagen y exportar a RGB 1024 × 1024 con `prepare.swift`. La generación es estocástica; los PNG versionados son la referencia visual final.

## Ajuste final de encuadre

Edit this icon by changing ONLY the framing. Preserve the cyan orbit and amber body artwork, lighting, colors and proportions exactly, but reduce the entire orbit and body together so their widest horizontal span occupies 72% of the full square canvas width. Keep the combined mark centered horizontally and vertically. Leave at least 14% dark navy margin on left and right, seamlessly extending the existing dark navy background. Keep edge-to-edge opaque square canvas, no corners, no frame, no text or added objects. Do not zoom in. The mark should be measurably smaller on the square, about 72% width. Render only one square icon.

## Prompt inicial

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: iOS app icon for an unofficial futuristic astronomy observatory app. Create ONE finished square icon, 1024x1024, opaque edge-to-edge square canvas, no rounded corners or outer frame. Concept: VECTOR ORBIT. One iconic thick tilted elliptical orbital trajectory, from bottom left to upper right, forming a large open asymmetrical loop, with a single large bright warm-white/amber spherical body on its upper-right endpoint. The body is visibly connected to the orbit. The ellipse is substantially thick (about 8% canvas) with a sculptural cyan-to-electric-blue gradient and subtle depth, a rounded cut endpoint at lower left and a purposeful open gap near the luminous body. Orbit plus sphere together occupy about 72% of canvas. The center of the orbit is pure negative space, no central planet or other object. Premium futuristic scientific instrument mark, strong clean geometric silhouette, beautifully restrained almost-vector 3D rendering, white/cyan highlights, deep dark navy background #070D1F. Exquisitely balanced bold graphic at 60px, not thin orbital hairlines, no rings encircling a planet. No text, letters, typography, logos, NASA insignia, flags, crosshairs, stars, lens, planet disc, extra orbit paths, clutter, little particles, app mockup, rounded corners, borders or preview sheet. Render only the square icon art.
