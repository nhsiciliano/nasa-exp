## Prompt inicial

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: iOS app icon for an unofficial futuristic astronomy observatory app. Create ONE finished square icon, 1024x1024, opaque edge-to-edge square canvas, no rounded corners or outer frame. Concept: OCULAR. One bold front-facing telescope optical lens centered in square, about 70% of canvas diameter. A substantial sculptural dark-metal chamfered housing catches strong icy cyan light. Inside it a wide luminous turquoise glass lens, concave with rich ultramarine depths and large smooth clean optical reflection shapes. A single crisp large asymmetric white/cyan four-point specular flare glints at the upper right of the glass, integrated into the lens, with a tiny warm amber caustic nearby. The lens is bright and visibly glass, NOT a dark empty hole. Modern precision optical instrument, premium sci-fi observatory art, beautifully polished 3D but restrained and iconic, simple concentric geometry with no screws, no writing, no intricate grooves or technical markings. Deep dark navy background #070D1F, cyan #2EE6FF, white, tiny warm amber accent. One clear hero motif filling 60–75% canvas, high contrast large shapes legible at 60px. No text, letters, typography, logos, NASA insignia, flags, stars in background, planetary horizons, orbital swooshes, app mockup, borders or preview sheet. Render only the square icon art.
