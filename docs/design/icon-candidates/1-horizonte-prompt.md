# 1-horizonte

Fuente editable: prompt usado con la herramienta integrada `imagegen`.
Generación independiente, sin imágenes de referencia y con fondo opaco.

Proceso: generar con el prompt inicial, aplicar el ajuste de encuadre sobre esa imagen y exportar a RGB 1024 × 1024 con `prepare.swift`. La generación es estocástica; los PNG versionados son la referencia visual final.

## Ajuste final de encuadre

Edit this icon by changing ONLY the framing. Preserve the planet artwork, lighting, colors and proportions exactly, but reduce the planet diameter to 72% of the full square canvas width. Keep centered horizontally and vertically. Leave 14% dark navy margin on all four sides between the disc and canvas edge, seamlessly extending the existing dark navy background. Keep edge-to-edge opaque square canvas, no corners, no frame, no text or added objects. Do not zoom in. The planet should be measurably smaller on the square, about 72% diameter including the visible disc. Render only one square icon.

## Prompt inicial

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: iOS app icon for an unofficial futuristic astronomy observatory app. Create ONE finished square icon, 1024x1024, opaque edge-to-edge square canvas, no rounded corners or outer frame. Concept: HORIZON. A single monumental planet disc centered in the square, diameter about 72% of canvas. The upper hemisphere is a broad luminous cyan crescent lit by a dramatic raking sunrise, with a white-hot thin inner rim and turquoise-to-deep-blue soft volumetric transition down into a dark but visible navy planet. A very small warm amber highlight is integrated along the upper right horizon, not a separate star. The disc remains visually circular and the lit crescent thick enough to read instantly at 60px. Elegant minimal dimensional sci-fi art, graphic bold sculptural silhouette, exceptional polish, no surface topography or tiny texture. Deep dark blue background approximately #070D1F, main accent #2EE6FF, white. Occupies 60–75% of canvas. Large bright visual mass and strong contrast, disciplined negative space. No text, letters, typography, logos, NASA insignia, flags, stars, crosshairs, orbit lines, extra objects, app mockup, borders, preview sheet, decorative tiny details. Render only the square icon art.
